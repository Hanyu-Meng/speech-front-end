ARCHITECTURE MAP

Architectures across tasks

GGenerative

Diffusion, flow matching, and codec/token LMs can restore naturalness, but require careful control of content hallucination, speaker drift, and sampling latency.

DDiscriminative

Masks, complex mapping, TasNet, and beamforming provide more controllable single-pass inference; their main risks are over-smoothing and out-of-domain mismatch.

HHybrid

Mapping or beamforming first removes distortion, then a generative model restores detail. The balance is stronger, but cascaded errors and system complexity increase.

METRIC CHEAT SHEET

Never read a metric in isolation

At minimum, report signal fidelity + perceptual quality + intelligibility/downstream performance + runtime cost. Add content and speaker consistency for generative models and TSE.
